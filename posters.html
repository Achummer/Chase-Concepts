Purpose and Intention

These are the promotional poster design iterations I went through before deciding on the final design I used for the film. I wanted the poster to evoke an ominous energy while also having a playful quality to it. To achieve this I utilized darker color palettes and simple stylized figures throughout each design. I started out with 5 ideas to make mockups for which you can see below.

Once these poster mockups were made, I sought advice from my peers on which ones they thought conveyed my intentions the best. With their help, I was able to narrow down my potential designs and continued refinements for them which you can see below.

As much as I liked the design with the house, I agreed with my peers that the design showcasing the two figures running was the best iteration for my intentions for the final poster. With a few more adjustments, the final poster was complete!
