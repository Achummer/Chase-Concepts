Film Location

CHASE was filmed in the backyard of my childhood house. I specifically waited till the sun was almost finished setting so that it would be dark out yet we would still have enough light to be able to see where we were going. All footage was captured on my smartphone.

Crew Involved

The people involved in playing the characters in CHASE were director Abby Hummer playing the main character, Kiara Knowles playing the horned creature, and Kael Knowles playing the hooded figure. Costuming was provided by the director as well as items the other actors brought from home. All editing was done by the director and the soundtrack for the film is comprised of royalty free music track “Dead Silence” by Lennon Hutton.
