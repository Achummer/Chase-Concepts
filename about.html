Purpose of Site

CHASE Concepts is a site dedicated to details and background information on the short film project made byAbby Hummer in her Introduction to Typography class last year. All images and video footage used comes directly from the creator of the film.

Film Summary

CHASE is a short film following our curious protagonist investigating a strange noise coming from outside her home in the middle of the night. While investigating, she is struck with the unfortunate reality there is more than birds and night creatures surrounding her home this night.

About Creator

I am the director, writer, and editor for the short film CHASE. I am a design student at the University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee and I made this film for a title sequence project in my Introduction to Typography class. For this project, students had to develop title logos/wordmarks and posters based on a film they produce themselves. I took this project as an opportunity to involve my niece and nephew in my school work since they were visiting the weekend I had to film.
