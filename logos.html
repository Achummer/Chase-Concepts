Purpose and Intention

For the main logo/wordmark of the film, I wanted to utilize text that had similar visual qualities to things seen within the short film. To do this I looked for typefaces that had more organic shaping and texture to trees or branches since there were a lot of trees and bushes in the film. I also looked for type that had a glitched or fuzzy texture to resemble the grain you see in old analog horror films. To start I did some type studies to begin searching for the ideal typeface. See Below

After the type studies, I took the fonts I felt had the most potential as the main logo/wordmark of my film then began editing the letterforms and adding pictorial elements to make proof of concept designs. See Below

After this, I sought out advice from peers to help me narrow down which logos to take to the coloring refinement stage. This would also help in determining what the poster would end up looking like since the logo was meant to be an integral part of the poster. See Below

With the logo ideas narrowed down to these 5, I proceeded to make the poster concepts (see Posters page). Through the refining and completion of the poster I also finalized the logo at the same time. The final logo uses slightly edited letterforms of CCExtreme Ruthlessly, 72 and an organic background shape made by myself. See Below
